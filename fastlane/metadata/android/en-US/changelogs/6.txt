Coffee 1.5.0

What's Changed
- Completely redesigned the Home, Onboarding, and About screens with a cleaner, more modern experience.
- Improved the timer controls and overall usability.
- Simplified permission handling, including better recovery when permissions are denied.
- Improved timer reliability when the device clock changes or Android restarts the background service.
- Reduced unnecessary background activity to improve battery usage and performance.
- Improved widgets, including dynamic colors on supported Android versions.
- Updated Android compatibility for newer devices.
- Removed the old "Nothing" variant and unused resources.
- Replaced the unlimited timer option with a safer 120-minute maximum.
- Various stability, reliability, and usability improvements.
