Coffee 1.6.0

What's New
- Added 10 more shapes, bringing the total to 35 unique design options.
- Redesigned the setup interface to place shape previews and color theme options side by side.

What's Changed
- Extracted and isolated the widget pinning logic.
- The core Coffee service now consistently launches in the foreground, ensuring the operating system does not accidentally terminate it during background tasks.

Bug Fixes & Reliability
- The app now persists and remembers your original system screen timeout. If your device restarts unexpectedly, your original settings are safely restored.
- Fixed an issue where newly placed Home Screen widgets would appear entirely transparent or fail to respond to touch inputs.
